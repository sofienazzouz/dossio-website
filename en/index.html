How it works

Three steps, that's all

  1. Scan the document

    A card, a receipt, a contract: take a photo of it, or import an image or a PDF.

  2. Confirm the dates

    The app reads the dates on your iPhone and suggests them. You pick the right one, or correct it.

  3. Get reminded

    A notification arrives before the deadline, or before the last day to cancel a contract. "Remind me tomorrow" if now isn't a good time.

Features

All the household paperwork, in one place

Papers

ID card, passport, driving licence, residence permit… with the expiry date and photos of the document. "I renewed this paper" restarts the reminders.

Warranties

The receipt, purchase date, warranty end, price and serial number. A bar shows how much time is left.

Contracts and subscriptions

Insurance, energy, internet, streaming: the renewal date and notice period you enter give the last day to cancel. Subscriptions roll forward on their own.

Reminders at the right time

You choose when to be warned (6 months, 1 month, 1 week before…). Add to Calendar, Siri and Shortcuts, iPhone search.

If you lose your wallet

A list of your cards with the number to call to block them, typed by you. One tap to call.

Private and protected

Face ID lock, screen hidden in the app switcher, files encrypted by iOS. Photos never go to your photo library.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Does the app tell me when to renew or cancel?

No. Dossio records the dates and notice periods you enter and reminds you of them. It gives no legal, administrative or financial advice. If in doubt, check with the organisation concerned.

Is the date reading reliable?

It helps, but it can be wrong. That's why you always confirm the date before saving. Check the original document.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. If you change phones, create a backup from the app's Settings, then restore it on the new one.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole app works offline, including scanning and date reading.

Is it free?

Yes: for one person and up to 15 items, the essentials are free and ad-free (scan, reminders, backup, Face ID lock…). Dossio+ adds family, unlimited items and extras, for a one-time payment of €2.99, no subscription. For the first month, everything is unlocked for free.

Which languages?

French and English. The app follows your iPhone's language, and you can change it in Settings.
