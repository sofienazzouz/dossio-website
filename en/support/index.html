Frequently asked questions

Does the app tell me how long my document is valid?

No. Dossio records the dates you type (or confirm after a scan) and reminds you of them. It gives no legal, administrative or financial advice: it doesn't say how long a document is valid, what a warranty covers or how to cancel a contract. If in doubt, check with the organisation concerned.

The scan read the wrong date.

Automatic reading can be wrong (blurry photo, damaged document, several dates on the page). Before saving, pick the right date or fix it by hand. Always check the original document. Scanning with the camera only works on a real iPhone or iPad; you can also import a photo.

I don't get the reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings › Dossio › Notifications. Also check that the item has a date and at least one reminder chosen. A Focus mode can hide notifications. For a contract with a notice period, the reminder comes before the cancellation deadline you entered, not before the renewal date.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

In the app, open Settings › Backup › Create a backup, and save the file (to Files, iCloud Drive, via AirDrop…). On the new iPhone, install Dossio and choose "Restore a backup" in the same place. This file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe. iPhone iCloud Backups also include the app's data.

Face ID doesn't recognise me.

If Face ID fails, the app offers your iPhone passcode. You can turn the lock off in the app's Settings. To turn it on, your iPhone needs a passcode.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the items in the app, or simply delete the app from your iPhone: all the data and documents it contains are erased. Also remember the backups and PDFs you exported.

Is it free?

Yes: for one person and up to 15 items, the essentials are free and ad-free (scan and date reading, reminders, "I renewed it" button, add to Calendar, Siri, backup, Face ID lock, "If you lose your wallet" list…). Dossio+ adds family profiles, unlimited items, PDFs (insurance claim file, folder per person), trip preparation, cost totals and the year calendar, for a one-time payment of €2.99, no subscription. For the first month, everything is unlocked for free. Already bought it on another iPhone? Open the Dossio+ screen in the app and tap "Restore my purchase".
