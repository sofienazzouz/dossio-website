Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short
  • Your papers, warranties, contracts and scanned documents stay on your iPhone.
  • No account, no server, no ads, no trackers.
  • We never receive, see or sell any of your data.

1. Who we are

The Dossio app is published by Sofien Azzouz ("we"). For any question about this policy: gymindconsulting@gmail.com.

2. The information you record

Depending on how you use it, the app stores only on your device:

  • the household members you add (a first name or name);
  • your papers, warranties, contracts and subscriptions: type, name, dates (expiry, purchase, warranty end, renewal, next payment), document or serial number, provider or shop, amount, frequency, notice period, the provider's phone number and website, notes and chosen reminders;
  • the photos, scans and PDFs you attach (ID documents, receipts, contracts…);
  • the "If you lose your wallet" list (card name, number to call, note);
  • your settings (language, appearance, lock, preferences).

This information is used only to make the app work. It is never sent to a server: Dossio doesn't have one. So we have no access to it. The app's files are protected by iOS encryption: they cannot be read while the iPhone is locked. Photos and scans are never saved to your photo library.

If you add another person's documents (partner, child…), they also stay on your device; make sure that person agrees.

3. Automatic document reading

When you scan a document, text recognition (to find dates, the document type or a receipt total) runs entirely on the iPhone, using Apple's tools. No image or text is sent over the internet. The result can be wrong: you always check and confirm before anything is saved.

4. iPhone permissions

  • Camera: to scan a document, when you ask.
  • Photos and Files: you pick the images or PDFs to import yourself with Apple's pickers; the app has no access to the rest of your photo library or files.
  • Notifications: for your reminders before a deadline. They are local notifications, scheduled on the device, with no server. They show the document's name; depending on your iOS settings, they may be visible on the lock screen.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: if you turn on the lock, to open the app (your iPhone passcode works too). Recognition is done by iOS; the app never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode, only "succeeded" or "failed".
  • Calendar: when you add a deadline, Apple's add-event screen opens and you confirm the event yourself. The app doesn't read your calendar.
  • Phone and browser: the "Call" and "Website" buttons open the call or Safari with the number or address you typed.

5. iPhone search and Siri

  • Spotlight search (off by default): if you turn it on, only the name, type and date of your items are added to iPhone search, on the device. Never numbers, notes or files. It is switched off automatically when the Face ID lock is on.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: shortcuts read your deadlines on the device. They give no information while the lock is on.

6. What leaves the device, and only if you decide

  • Backup: you can export a file containing all your data and attached documents, and choose where to put it (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email…). This file is not encrypted by the app: keep it somewhere safe.
  • PDFs: the insurance claim file and the per-person folder are only shared when you choose where to send them.
  • iCloud sync and family sharing (Dossio+, off by default): if you turn it on, your data and attached documents are copied to your iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), for your other devices and for the people you invite yourself. The content of each item is stored in CloudKit encrypted fields, with keys from your iCloud Keychain; photos and PDFs are encrypted by Apple. Dossio has no server and can't read this data. You can stop syncing, leave a family or delete the iCloud copy at any time (Settings › iCloud & family).
  • Document pack: the watermarked PDF is created on the iPhone and only shared when you choose where to send it.
  • Widget: it only shows the name and date of the next deadlines, and nothing while the lock is on.
  • Apple iCloud Backups: if they are turned on on your iPhone, they may include the app's data, according to your Apple settings.

Once a copy is shared, it depends on the service or person receiving it.

7. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party data collection kit. We don't sell or share any data. If you agreed to it in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports; they don't contain your documents.

8. When you email us

If you contact us by email, we use your address and message only to reply to you, and we delete them when the conversation is over (after 3 years at the latest). Never email us a copy of your ID documents: we don't need them.

9. Purchases

Dossio+ is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, fully handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details or your identity: the app only checks with Apple that the purchase was made.

10. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You can delete an item, an attached document or a person in the app, or delete the app to erase everything. Exported backups and PDFs, iCloud Backups and the iCloud sync copy (Settings › iCloud & family › Delete from iCloud) must be deleted separately.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability. Because your data is only on your device, you exercise them directly in the app (edit, delete, export a backup). For emails you sent us, write to us. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr), or your local authority.

12. Children

The app requires no account and collects no data from children or adults: everything stays on the device of the person using it.

13. This website

This website is a static page, with no cookies, no forms and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process technical connection data (such as the IP address) to deliver and secure the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

14. Changes

If the way the app works changes, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
